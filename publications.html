Research Statement

My research and expertise focus on food safety, environmental sustainability, and fermentation to develop innovative bioproducts while reducing waste. I integrate microbial fermentation and omics-based strategies to transform food waste into high-value compounds such as bacteriocins and nootropic therapeutics, contributing to a circular economy.

My work in environmental toxicology has also explored water quality, contaminant monitoring, and microbial ecology using next-generation sequencing and advanced analytical techniques. By bridging food science, biotechnology, and environmental stewardship, my goal is to support sustainable innovation and regulatory advancement.

Research Publications

1. Flavor or Safety? A Critical Review of Managing Processing-Induced Toxicants in Flavored Vegetable Oils

Food Control (2026)

This review examined the formation of harmful processing contaminants in flavoured vegetable oils and the trade-off between flavour development and food safety. It highlighted how high-temperature processing can generate compounds such as polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, 3-MCPD esters, and glycidyl esters. These findings emphasize the need to optimize roasting, refining, and flavouring conditions to produce high-quality oils while minimizing potential health risks.

2. Advancing Ethanol Quality Amidst a Pandemic: A Case Study from Saskatchewan

Canadian Journal of Chemistry (2025)

This study explores how Saskatchewan’s ethanol industry adapted during the COVID-19 pandemic to upgrade fuel-grade ethanol for hand sanitizer production. The Saskatchewan Structural Sciences Centre developed quality-assurance methods using ozonation, chemical treatments, carbon filtration, and optimized distillation to meet Health Canada standards. This initiative fostered collaboration between academia, government, and industry, turning a crisis into an opportunity for scientific innovation and economic sustainability.

3. Chemical Property, Bioactive Constituent and Volatile Compound Changes of Beef Tallow During an Improved Flavor-Retaining Refining Approach

Authors: Zhou, L., Zheng, M., Zhang, T., Wang, S., Jiang, X., Li, B., Hu, Z., Tse, T. J., Reaney, M. J. T., Nie, Q., He, D., Hu, C., Zheng, J., Lei, F., Zhang, Q.

Food Chemistry (2025)

This study introduces an innovative refining technique for beef tallow that preserves its unique aroma while removing impurities using enzymatic degumming, alkali deacidification, and adsorbent bleaching. The process effectively reduced phospholipids, free fatty acids, pigments, and cholesterol while minimally altering the flavour profile.

4. Production of α-Glycerylphosphorylcholine in Fermented Roots, Tubers, and Fruits

Authors: Tse, T. J., Chicilo, F., Wiens, D. J., Shen, J., Anleu Alegria, J., Kim, Y. J., Hong, J. Y., Kim, J. K., Shin, E-C., Reaney, M. J. T., Shim, Y. Y.

Foods (2024)

This study explored the fermentation of select root, tuber, and fruit crops with Saccharomyces cerevisiae to co-produce bioethanol and the nootropic compound α-glycerylphosphorylcholine (α-GPC). A strong correlation between methanol and α-GPC production suggests a potential biosynthetic link, highlighting both the biofuel and value-added potential of these feedstocks.

5. Transesterification Catalyst Strength as Observed by Solution-Based Oxygen-17 Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy

Authors: Purdy, S. K., Shen, J., Zhu, J., Tse, T. J., Reaney, M. J. T., Nie, Q., He, D., Hu, C., Zheng, J., Lei, F., Zhang, Q.

Canadian Journal of Chemistry (2024)

This study utilized natural-abundance 17O-NMR spectroscopy to analyze homogeneous transesterification catalysts for fatty acid methyl ester production. Alkoxide catalysts exhibited downfield shifts in 17O-NMR spectra that correlated with catalyst ion concentration. Potassium methoxide demonstrated the strongest electron deshielding and fastest catalytic activity.

6. Utilizing Nano-Adsorbents and Electrostatic Field Treatment for Sustainable Refinement of Crude Canola Oil

Authors: Zhou, L., Tse, T. J., Chicilo, F., Shen, J., Meda, V., Reaney, M. J. T.

Foods (2024)

This study explored a sustainable approach to refining crude canola oil by combining nano-adsorbents and electrostatic field treatment to remove impurities. The method minimized waste and water use, with nano-adsorbents successfully recycled for three refining cycles, highlighting its potential as an eco-friendly alternative to conventional refining.

7. Determination of Vanillin Compounds in Oilseed Using Solid Phase Extraction with Ultra-Performance Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry

Authors: Wang, S., Yang, Y., Tse, T. J., Reaney, M. J. T., Tu, F., Chen, Z., Fang, H., Kang, C., Jiang, X., Zhou, L., Wang, Y.

Journal of Food Composition and Analysis (2024)

This study developed a solid-phase extraction and UPLC-MS/MS method to identify and quantify vanillin, methylvanillin, and ethylvanillin in oilseeds. Analysis confirmed that vanillin in rapeseed oil originates from raw rapeseed. This method provides a reliable tool for supporting authenticity and quality assessment of oilseed-derived products.

8. Water-Modification of Micro- and Nano-Adsorbents: A Strategy for Enhancing Biodiesel Refining Efficiency

Authors: Zhou, L., Tse, T. J., Shen, J., Meda, V., Reaney, M. J. T.

Energy Conversion and Management: X (2024)

This study compared nanometre-scale and micrometre-scale silica and alumina adsorbents for refining crude fatty acid methyl esters. While nano-adsorbents created challenges related to product recovery and FAME losses, the results demonstrated opportunities to improve biodiesel purification while reducing reliance on extensive wet-washing processes.

9. Foaming with Starch: Exploring Faba Bean Aquafaba as a Green Alternative

Authors: Ramos-Figueroa, J. S., Tse, T. J., Shen, J., Purdy, S. K., Kim, J. K., Kim, Y. J., Han, B. K., Hong, J. Y., Shim, Y. Y., Reaney, M. J. T.

Foods (2023)

This study explored faba bean process water as a sustainable alternative to aquafaba for foaming applications in food. The isolated foam-inducing component retained foaming capacity, with enzymatic treatments confirming the role of both macromolecules. These findings highlight faba bean-derived ingredients as a promising plant-based substitute for egg whites in food formulations.

10. Electrostatic Field as an Emergent Technology in Refining Crude Vegetable Oils: A Review

Authors: Li, Z., Tse, T. J., Chicilo, F., Meda, V., Reaney, M. J. T.

Critical Reviews in Food Science and Nutrition (2023)

This review examined conventional and emerging refining technologies for vegetable oils and fatty acid esters. Nano-adsorbents and electrostatic field technologies showed potential for efficiently removing contaminants while minimizing oil loss, although recovery of spent nano-adsorbents remains a challenge. Electrostatic treatment represents a promising sustainable alternative for oil purification.

11. Uptake of Flaxseed Dietary Linusorbs Modulates Regulatory Genes Including Induction of Heat Shock Proteins and Apoptosis

Authors: Shim, Y. Y., Tse, T. J., Saini, A. K., Kim, Y. J., Reaney, M. J. T.

Foods (2022)

This study explored the bioactivity of linusorbs, cyclic peptides found in flaxseed, including their immunosuppressive and pro-apoptotic effects. Linusorbs were absorbed following flaxseed consumption and influenced heat-shock proteins and regulatory genes, highlighting their potential as biologically active compounds.

12. A Review of Flaxseed Lignan and the Extraction and Refinement of Secoisolariciresinol Diglucoside

Authors: Hu, Y., Tse, T. J., Shim, Y. Y., Purdy, S. K., Kim, Y. J., Meda, V., Reaney, M. J. T.

Critical Reviews in Food Science and Nutrition (2022)

This review highlighted the challenges associated with commercial-scale isolation of the flaxseed lignan secoisolariciresinol diglucoside and the need for optimized extraction and purification methods. Improved methodologies could expand lignan applications in medicine, dietary supplements, and functional foods.

13. Depletion of Cyanogenic Glycosides in Whole Flaxseed via Lactobacillaceae Fermentation

Authors: Huang, C., Tse, T. J., Purdy, S. K., Shen, J., Meda, V., Reaney, M. J. T.

Food Chemistry (2022)

This study investigated fermentation of whole flaxseed with Lactobacillaceae cultures to remove cyanogenic glycosides. The process eliminated cyanogenic glycosides while preserving fatty-acid composition and enhancing flaxseed oil and lignan concentrations, supporting expanded use in food, health, and animal-feed applications.

14. Availability of Bioactive Flax Lignan from Foods and Supplements

Authors: Tse, T. J., Guo, Y., Shim, Y. Y., Purdy, S. K., Kim, H. J., Cho, J. Y., Alcorn, J., Reaney, M. J. T.

Critical Reviews in Food Science and Nutrition (2022)

This review explored the lipid-lowering effects of flaxseed lignan secoisolariciresinol diglucoside and its polymer. Following consumption, lignan metabolites accumulate in tissues and may reduce cholesterol, triglycerides, and lipid peroxidation, supporting their potential use in functional foods and health products.

Journal of Cleaner Production (2022)

This study examined electrostatic field treatment as a sustainable alternative to traditional vegetable-oil refining. The method reduced phospholipids, free fatty acids, and peroxides without significantly altering carotenoid content or fatty-acid composition, while maintaining minimal oil loss.

This review examined risks associated with alcohol-based hand rubs formulated using technical-grade ethanol during the COVID-19 pandemic. Key concerns included methanol, ethyl acetate, and acetaldehyde contamination, highlighting the need for strong quality control and regulatory oversight.
