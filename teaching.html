Teaching, Mentorship & Leadership

I have contributed to undergraduate teaching, guest lectures, student supervision, research mentorship, and professional staff development across academic and industry laboratory environments. My approach emphasizes clear communication, practical application, critical thinking, and connecting scientific concepts with real-world research, regulatory, and industry challenges.

At the Saskatchewan Research Council, I supervised a multidisciplinary laboratory team of up to nine staff members. My responsibilities included assigning and reviewing technical work, supporting employee development, providing scientific guidance, managing laboratory priorities, and helping maintain quality and operational requirements within an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited environment.

I have also supported students and early-career professionals through guest lecturing, research supervision, technical training, and individualized mentorship. I strive to create an inclusive and supportive learning environment where individuals feel comfortable asking questions, developing new skills, and taking ownership of their professional growth.

Teaching & Technical Training Areas

My teaching and training experience bridges theoretical knowledge with applied laboratory, research, and industry practices. Areas of expertise include:

  • Food Chemistry, Agricultural Byproducts & Bioproduct Development
  • Food Safety, Microbiology & Molecular Testing
  • Environmental Toxicology & Environmental Monitoring
  • Chromatography, Mass Spectrometry & Analytical Methodologies
  • qPCR, ddPCR, Next-Generation Sequencing & Biomolecular Analysis
  • Laboratory Quality Management & ISO/IEC 17025 Requirements
  • Research Design, Scientific Writing & Grant Development
  • Laboratory Operations, Staff Training & Technical Project Management

Leadership & Mentorship Approach

  • Practical and Applied Learning: I use real-world research, laboratory, regulatory, and industry examples to help learners understand how technical concepts are applied in practice.
  • Supportive Team Development: I provide clear expectations, constructive feedback, and individualized guidance to help staff and students strengthen their technical capabilities and professional confidence.
  • Quality and Accountability: I emphasize accurate documentation, scientific integrity, quality assurance, regulatory awareness, and consistent adherence to established procedures.
  • Interdisciplinary Collaboration: I encourage open communication and knowledge-sharing among researchers, laboratory personnel, industry partners, and other project stakeholders.
  • Continuous Improvement: I support ongoing learning, process improvement, and the adoption of emerging technologies and best practices in research and laboratory operations.
