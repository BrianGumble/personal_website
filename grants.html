Grants Held and Contributions

The following grants represent successful research funding applications to which I contributed through project development, proposal writing, literature review, methodology development, budgeting, partnership coordination, reviewer responses, and editorial support.

Year Funding Agency Project Title Project Focus
2023 MITACS Globalink Research Award1 Cholinergic Bioactives from Cereals Investigated cholinergic bioactive compounds derived from cereal grains and their potential nutritional and health-related applications.
2021 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund1 Modular Purification Processes for Upgrading and Enriching Ethanol Developed modular purification approaches to upgrade ethanol and improve the efficiency and value of ethanol-processing streams.
2021 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund2 Industrial Products from Vegetable Oils Explored the conversion of vegetable oils into value-added industrial products and bio-based materials.
2020 MITACS Accelerate1 Adding Value to Canola Oilseed Developed processing and product-development strategies to increase the nutritional, functional, and economic value of canola.
2020 MITACS Accelerate1 Solid Fermentation of Barley Evaluated solid-state fermentation as a method to enhance the nutritional and functional properties of barley-derived materials.
2020 MITACS Accelerate2 Oilseed with Depleted Glycosides Investigated processing methods to reduce undesirable glycosides in oilseed-derived ingredients and products.
2020 MITACS Accelerate1 Upgrading Locally Available Sanitizer Ingredients for Health Services in Canada Supported the development and validation of sanitizer ingredients for healthcare and public health applications in Canada.
2020 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund1 Biodegradable Plastics from Canola and Pulses Developed biodegradable plastic materials using canola, pulses, and agricultural processing byproducts.
2019 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund1 Production of Phospholipids from the Ethanol Co-product GPC and Crop Oils Developed processes to produce phospholipids from glycerophosphocholine and crop-oil processing streams.
2019 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund2 Mass Production of Canola Phenolic Compounds for Health, Consumer, and Industrial Applications Advanced the extraction and scale-up of canola-derived phenolic compounds for health, consumer, and industrial markets.
2018 NSERC Discovery2 Electrostatic Conditioning of Vegetable Oil and Biodiesel Investigated electrostatic conditioning approaches to improve the processing and performance of vegetable oils and biodiesel.
2018 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund2 Electrostatic Conditioning of Vegetable Oil and Biodiesel Evaluated electrostatic processing technologies for improving vegetable-oil and biodiesel quality and functionality.
2018 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund2 Practical Oilseed Protein Products Developed practical protein ingredients and products derived from oilseed-processing streams.
2018 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund2 Oilseed with Depleted Glycosides and Metals Investigated methods for reducing glycosides and metals in oilseed-derived materials.
2018 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund2 Oil Emulsifier from Chickpea Developed chickpea-derived emulsifiers for food, ingredient, and industrial applications.
2017 Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund1 Insect Protein and Fat from Thin Stillage and Canola Meal Evaluated the conversion of thin stillage and canola meal into insect-derived protein and fat ingredients.

Contribution Categories

1 I developed the initial proposal, including defining the project scope, managing the letter of intent, coordinating contributions from collaborators and industry partners, and addressing reviewer feedback.

2 I contributed editorial and technical support, including literature reviews, methodology descriptions, proposal revisions, and responses to reviewer feedback.
